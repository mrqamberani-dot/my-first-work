About the Academy

Welcome to Ahmed Baloch Cricket Academy for Under 14, Kharan's premier youth cricket development center. Founded with a vision to nurture young talent in Balochistan, our academy provides professional coaching, physical conditioning, and tactical learning for aspiring young players under the age of 14.

Under the guidance of experienced coaches, we focus on fundamental cricket skills, team discipline, sportsmanship, and mental toughness to prepare local talent for district, provincial, and national trials.

Our U-14 Training Programs

Batting Masterclass

Technique development, stance improvement, shot selection, and facing fast vs spin bowling in net practice.

Bowling Fundamentals

Specialized coaching for fast bowlers (run-up, action, seam position) and spin bowlers (flight, turn, control).

Fielding & Fitness

Agility drills, catching techniques, ground fielding, throwing accuracy, and stamina building for U-14 boys.

Match Practice

Regular internal practice matches, scenario training, and local competitive matches in Kharan.
